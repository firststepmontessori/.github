# Community conduct

We want project discussions to be calm, constructive and safe.

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Discuss the work, not the person.
- Protect the privacy of children, families and staff.
- Do not publish personal information, harassment or discriminatory content.
- Raise factual concerns with evidence and without speculation.

Organization maintainers may edit, hide or remove material that conflicts with
these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community.
