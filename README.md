# First Step Montessori School

First Step Montessori School is an early-childhood education centre in
Vidyaranyapura, North Bengaluru. Established in 2025, the school offers little
learners a warm, carefully prepared environment for purposeful activity,
discovery and growing independence.

The school community values respectful guidance, hands-on learning, steady
daily rhythms and close partnership with families. Its public representation
protects the dignity and privacy of every child: no child names or photographs
are published without an appropriate safeguarding and consent process.
