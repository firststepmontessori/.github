# First Step Montessori School

## A gentle beginning for curious little minds

First Step Montessori School is an early-childhood education centre established
in 2025 in Vidyaranyapura, North Bengaluru, Karnataka 560097.

## A thoughtfully prepared environment

The Montessori environment is arranged with care so little learners can move,
choose meaningful activities, practise everyday skills and discover ideas at a
comfortable pace. Calm order and hands-on materials help children develop
concentration, coordination, confidence and independence.

## Learning through everyday discovery

Children are encouraged to learn by doing: caring for their surroundings,
working with purposeful materials, communicating with others and returning to
activities until they feel capable. Adults observe closely, guide respectfully
and make room for each child's natural curiosity.

## A caring rhythm for young children

The school brings learning, play, rest and care together in a familiar setting.
Its programmes and daycare offering are intended to support children through
the important early years while giving families a warm, approachable school
community close to home.

## Partnership with families

Families are invited to understand the Montessori environment, speak directly
with the school and take part in a respectful relationship centred on their
child's well-being and development. Programme details, timings and admissions
information are shared only after they are confirmed by the school.

## Child safety and privacy

Every child deserves dignity and privacy. The school's public channels do not
publish child names or photographs without an appropriate safeguarding and
parental-consent process. Personal information about children and families is
never used merely for promotion.
