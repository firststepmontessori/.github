# First Step Montessori School

First Step Montessori School is an early-years Montessori school established
in 2025 in Vidyaranyapura, North Bengaluru, Karnataka 560097.

We are building a calm, welcoming digital home that helps prospective families
understand the school, its Montessori approach, programmes, daycare offering,
admissions process, and location.

## Our website project

The school website is being donated and developed in public using
[Astro](https://astro.build/) and [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/).
One shared, accessible codebase supports two visual directions:

- **Calm Prepared Garden** — warm, natural and quietly structured.
- **Joyful Geometry** — bright, playful and composed from simple shapes.

Both directions support System, Light and Night colour modes, subtle
reduced-motion-safe animation, semantic HTML and local-search foundations.

[Explore the website repository →](https://github.com/firststepmontessori/firststepmontessori)

## Child safety and privacy

The first release uses illustrations and geometric artwork only. It contains no
child photographs, child names, parent details, media uploads, gallery, public
enquiry form, tracking pixels or marketing cookies. Any future use of child
media must follow a documented consent and safeguarding process.

## Content integrity

Public school facts are verified before publication. Unconfirmed accreditation,
founder-employment history, fees, schedules and programme details are not
presented as facts.
